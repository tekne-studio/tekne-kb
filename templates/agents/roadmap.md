### Roadmap

- **What comes next:** `kb roadmap`, in order. A task goes to {{roadmap}}: a wish is one line under {{later}}; what comes next is a `###` item under {{now}}, in its place in the order, with its {{doneWhen}} line.
- What waits on someone is an open question with `--unblocks {{item}}`; the page keeps no blocked section.
- An item leaves the page in the commit that meets its {{doneWhen}} line, and what it built lives in the topic doc; dropping one is a decision that says why. The method: `kb guide roadmap`.
